I came to [m-industries](http://m-industries.com) through my final bachelor project: building an Alan IDE in Alan. To make that happen we also worked on general improvements to Alan as a job, and I liked it pretty much :)

The company is specialized in model-driven programming, where the data and how that data can be manipulated determine the shape of the problem, and thus the solution. That is a new way of programming and problem solving, which is always an awesome challenge!
