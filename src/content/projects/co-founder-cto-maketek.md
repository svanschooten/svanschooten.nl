At [MakeTek](https://maketek.nl) we specialized in quick design and development rounds, so customers could test their ideas and iterate faster. To do this we used Node.js-based building blocks that we could re-use.

As co-founder and CTO I was responsible for the tech decisions, and I did most of the development during the startup phase.
